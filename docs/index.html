Methodological Foundations

The operations research prototypes in this repository demonstrate practical applications of a rigorous mathematical toolkit. While these use-cases focus on operational workflows, the underlying methodologies rely on the same mathematical, statistical, and computational models I have published in peer-reviewed journals:

Constrained Optimization & Advanced Probability

Workflow optimization relies on mathematical programming. My work applies constrained optimization (including linear and quadratic programming, and projection on polyhedral sets), simulated annealing heuristics, Markov chains, probability calculus, and maximum entropy models.

  • Peer-Reviewed Applications: [7], [8]

Network Optimization & Graph Algorithms

Graph theory and network analysis are fundamental to operations research. My published work in this area includes analytical approximations for probabilities of edge-counts conditional to degrees, solutions to unipartite and bipartite generalized degree sequence problems (determining maximum graph size upon edge-rewiring given prescribed vertex degrees), algorithms with optimal time complexity, unsupervised clustering in large networks based on degree robust measures, and identification of community structures.

  • Peer-Reviewed Applications: [4], [13], [14], [15]

Stochastic Modeling & Nonlinear Dynamical Systems

Modeling operations under uncertainty relies on stochastic processes and dynamical systems. My published work includes globally convergent iterative methods for nonlinear algebraic systems, stochastic linear and nonlinear differential equations, partial differential equations (PDEs), bifurcation analysis, and first passage time problems in the context of coherence resonance for excitable systems.

  • Peer-Reviewed Applications: [19], [21], [20], [22], [23]

Multivariate Statistics & Large-Scale Data Analysis

Extracting reliable signals from noisy data is fundamental to descriptive and predictive analytics. My published work applies multivariate statistical approaches, Monte Carlo-based permutation testing, and reverse engineering methodologies to large-scale datasets.

  • Peer-Reviewed Applications: [9], [11], [12]
